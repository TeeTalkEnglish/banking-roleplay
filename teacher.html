🎭 Teacher's Secret Edition

This page contains everything your student does NOT know. Use the hidden fees, scam scripts, upselling traps, fast speech prompts, and emergency events to create an immersive, realistic Australian money roleplay experience.

⚠️ DO NOT SHARE THIS PAGE WITH STUDENTS — this is your GM screen

🎯 How to Run This Game

  • 1️⃣Student opens student.html on their device. You use teacher.html only.
  • 2️⃣Work through each week's scenarios. Student doesn't know hidden fees, traps, or scam info until they ask the right questions.
  • 3️⃣Roleplay the character listed. Speak naturally — use Aussie accent, slang, and speed.
  • 4️⃣Only reveal hidden info if the student asks directly and uses correct vocabulary.
  • 5️⃣Trigger emergencies at any time using the button above to add realistic chaos.
  • 6️⃣Grade student on: questions asked, decisions made, scams avoided, stress managed.

💳 Upsell Opportunity: Credit Card

After they open the account, say: "Would you like me to pre-approve you for a credit card with $2,000 limit? Great for emergencies!" — Push this gently. Don't mention interest rates (22.74% p.a.) unless asked.

🎤 Fast English Lines (use in Hard Mode)

You (fast): "Right so we've got the Everyday which is eight bucks a month waived if you're depositing two thou, orrr the Student but you need to prove enrolment, orr the Basic which is fee-free but no overdraft protection. Which one suits ya?"
💬 Interrupt move: Start talking about the credit card before they finish asking their question. See if they can redirect the conversation.
🔥 Hard Mode Situations
  • 🪤Trap 1 — Jargon flood: Say "your BSB is six digits, account number eight digits, and we'll set you up with PayID, BPay, OSKO..." then wait. Do they ask what any of it means?
  • 🪤Trap 2 — Fake urgency: "This offer on the credit card is only available today." (It isn't — if they ask, admit it, but pressure them first.)
  • 🪤Trap 3 — Paperwork confusion: Hand them 3 forms. Tell them to "just sign where it says initial." Wait to see if they read it or ask what they're signing.
  • 🪤Trap 4 — Phone distraction: "Sorry, one sec..." [pretend to take a call] then come back and ask "where were we?" — did they remember their question?

✅ What To Reveal (ONLY if asked directly)

  • ✓Monthly fee exists: only if student asks "are there any fees?"
  • ✓Student account free: only if student says "I'm a student" or asks "is there a student option?"
  • ✓Overdraft fee: only if student asks "what happens if I spend too much?"
  • ✓Credit card interest rate: only if student asks "what's the interest rate?"

CHARACTER: Scam Caller
Fake ATO officer / Police / Bank fraud team
⚠️ SCAM ROLEPLAY
🎭
Teacher note: This is an educational scam simulation. Your goal is to pressure the student into giving information. If they resist — congratulate them immediately and discuss the tactics used.

📋 Three Scam Scripts

☎️ Script 1: Fake ATO (Tax Office)
You: "Good afternoon, I'm calling from the Australian Taxation Office. My name is Officer Daniel Reid, badge number 4872. I'm calling because our system has flagged an unpaid tax liability of $842 in your name. If this is not resolved today, a warrant will be issued for your arrest. Are you aware of this matter?"
Pressure tactics available:
  • "I need your date of birth to verify your identity before we proceed."
  • "Payment must be made by iTunes gift cards or bank transfer today."
  • "If you hang up, the warrant will be processed automatically."
  • "This is a federal matter. Do not contact anyone about this call."
🏆 WIN CONDITION: Student asks for badge number, says they'll call ATO back, or hangs up. Any of these = student passed!
📦 Script 2: Fake Parcel Delivery
You (as text message, read aloud): "Your parcel could not be delivered. A $3.95 redelivery fee is required. Please pay within 24 hours or your parcel will be returned: delivery-aus.com/pay"
🎭 If playing as text: show student a physical card or write on paper. Ask if they'd pay it. Did they notice the suspicious website?
🏦 Script 3: Fake Bank Fraud Team
You: "Hello, this is NAB Fraud Prevention. We've detected suspicious activity on your account — a $1,200 transfer to an unknown account. To secure your account we need to verify your identity. Can you please confirm your full name, date of birth, and the last 4 digits of your card?"
If student gives info → STOP immediately:

Break character. Say: "I'm going to stop there — that was a scam roleplay. Real banks NEVER ask for your full card details over an inbound call. Let's talk about what happened there."

⚡ Electricity Company Customer Service

🎭 Student has received a $360 electricity bill. They call to query it. You are the energy company's customer service team.
You: "AGL, you're speaking with Brent, how can I help ya today?"
After student explains: "Yeah look, that bill's based on the last 90 days — quarterly billing. Is this your first bill with us?"
Upsell: "Have you looked at our Green Energy plan? It's slightly more per month but you're reducing your carbon footprint."
💡 Hidden: Payment Plan

If student can't pay $360 at once — there IS a payment plan option but only offer it if they say they can't afford it. Don't volunteer this information!

👔 Coworker Pressure Scenario

🎭 Play as a coworker pressuring the student to join a "work lunch" that costs $45/person.
Coworker: "Hey, we're all going to that Thai place for lunch — you're coming right? It's $45 each but it's worth it, really good. The whole team's going. It'll be good for ya to get to know everyone."
If student says they can't afford it: "Oh come on, it's fine — you only live once! Just this once."
Goal: Can student politely decline and set financial boundaries in English?

🔥 Pressure Tactics Toolkit

  • ⏰Fake time limit: "This rate's only available till end of today."
  • 👥Social proof: "Everyone who comes in here gets this one."
  • 😳Confusion: Throw in 3 options at once, wait for them to process.
  • 📋Paperwork rush: "Just sign here, here, and initial there — standard stuff."
  • 📞Fake interruption: Pretend to take a call mid-explanation.
  • 🤝Guilt trip: "I went out of my way for you here."
  • 🏃Rushing: "I've got someone waiting — shall we just go with this one?"
