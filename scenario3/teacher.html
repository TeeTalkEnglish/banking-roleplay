Day 3 Teacher Guide

International Money Transfers

Students must discover information through questioning. Do not give all information immediately.

Standard Information For All Scenarios

  • Transfer Fee: AUD $15
  • Transfer Time: 1–3 Business Days
  • Maximum Standard Transfer: AUD $10,000
  • Photo ID Required
  • SWIFT Code Required
  • Recipient Name Required
  • Bank Name Required
  • Account Number Required

Scenario 3A: Family Transfer

Teller Role: Help a student send AUD $500 to Vietnam.

Objectives:

  • Verify identity.
  • Explain the transfer fee clearly.
  • Explain exchange rate and local currency conversion.
  • Explain processing time.
  • Check recipient information carefully.

Suggested Teller Response:

  • "May I please see some photo identification so I can start the transfer?"
  • "The transfer fee is AUD $15, so if you send AUD $500 today you will pay AUD $515 in total."
  • "At today’s rate, AUD $500 would convert to about 8,000,000 Vietnamese đồng."
  • "The money should arrive in Vietnam in 1 to 3 business days, which means if we send it today it may arrive by Thursday or Friday."
  • "Please make sure the recipient's name, bank name, account number, and SWIFT code are complete before we process it."

Scenario 3B: Missing SWIFT Code

Teller Role: Customer wants to transfer money but is missing information.

Objectives:

  • Explain what a SWIFT code is.
  • Explain why it is required.
  • Help customer obtain it.

Suggested Teller Response:

  • "A SWIFT code is a special number that identifies the recipient's bank overseas, and we need it to send the money correctly."
  • "Without the SWIFT code, I cannot process the transfer even if you have the rest of the details."
  • "For example, if you want to send AUD $500, I need the SWIFT code before I can move the money on your behalf."
  • "Please contact your recipient or their bank for that code, and then we can complete the transfer without another visit."

Correct Outcome:

Transfer cannot proceed until the SWIFT code is provided.

Scenario 3C: Wrong Account Number

Teller Role: Customer has incorrect account information.

Objectives:

  • Identify account mismatch.
  • Prevent transfer.
  • Request corrected details.

Suggested Teller Response:

  • "I need to confirm the account number carefully before I send AUD $500 to make sure it arrives at the right account."
  • "If the account number is wrong, the transfer could be delayed while we trace it, or the money could be returned."
  • "For example, if you give 12345678 instead of 12345679, the payment may be rejected or returned after several business days."
  • "Please double-check the numbers and give me the correct account number so I can process it now."

Correct Outcome:

Transfer is paused until correct information is supplied.

Scenario 3D: Emergency Transfer

Teller Role: Customer needs money delivered urgently.

Objectives:

  • Discuss faster transfer options.
  • Explain extra fees.
  • Set realistic expectations.

Suggested Teller Response:

  • "Why do you need the money urgently, and how quickly does it need to arrive?"
  • "There is a faster option available, but it will cost extra on top of the standard AUD $15 fee."
  • "For example, if you want the transfer sent urgently, the total cost may be AUD $535 for a AUD $500 transfer."
  • "Even with express service, the money may take up to 1 business day, depending on whether the receiving bank processes urgent payments immediately."
  • "I need to check the recipient details and confirm whether the destination bank can receive the urgent payment today."

Correct Outcome:

Customer understands delivery times and costs.

Secret Challenge Cards

Challenge 1: Customer forgot passport.
Challenge 2: Customer only knows recipient name.
Challenge 3: Customer has insufficient funds.
Challenge 4: Receiving bank is closed due to public holiday.
Challenge 5: Customer wants to send AUD $20,000.
Challenge 6: Fraud monitoring triggers additional questions.
Challenge 7: Customer enters incorrect recipient name.
Challenge 8: Customer becomes frustrated about fees.

Suggested Teller Language

  • How may I help you today?
  • May I see some photo identification?
  • Could you confirm the recipient details?
  • I'll just verify that information.
  • The transfer fee is AUD $15.
  • The transfer should arrive within 1–3 business days.
  • Unfortunately I cannot process the transfer without that information.
  • Would you like me to explain the process?
  • Is there anything else I can help you with today?

Assessment Checklist

  • ☐ Student greeted appropriately.
  • ☐ Student explained purpose clearly.
  • ☐ Student asked follow-up questions.
  • ☐ Student requested important information.
  • ☐ Student used banking vocabulary.
  • ☐ Student solved the problem.
  • ☐ Student completed the transfer process.
  • ☐ Student communicated professionally.
