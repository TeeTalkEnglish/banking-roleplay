🔍 Real-Life Research Challenge — Find Your Award

Imagine today is your first week working at an Australian meat processing factory. Your supervisor says,

"You're covered by an Award. Please make sure you understand your pay."

Your mission is to research your employment conditions using official Australian websites. Do not reveal the answer until you have searched for it yourself. After completing each task, explain your answer to your teacher in English.

🥩 Task 1 — Which Award Covers Your Job?

Find the Award that covers meat processing workers in Australia.

💡 Need a Hint?

Search Google: Fair Work Meat Industry Award

✅ Reveal Answer

The correct Award is the Meat Industry Award (MA000059). It covers many employees working in abattoirs, boning rooms, meat processing plants and packing operations.

💰 Task 2 — Find the Current Hourly Rate

Find the minimum hourly pay for a Level 1 employee.

💡 Need a Hint?

Open the latest Fair Work Pay Guide and find: Level 1 — Ordinary Hourly Rate

✅ Reveal Answer

There isn't one fixed answer. The hourly rate changes every year. Always use the latest Fair Work Pay Guide.

⏰ Task 3 — When Do Overtime Rates Apply?

Research how overtime is paid.

💡 Need a Hint?

Search inside the Award for: Overtime Look for:

  • Time and a half
  • Double time

✅ Reveal Answer

Employees are usually paid a higher rate for overtime. Common overtime rates include:

  • Time and a half (1.5×)
  • Double time (2×)
The exact rules depend on the Award and when the overtime is worked.

📅 Task 4 — Sunday & Public Holiday Pay

Find the penalty rate for working on Sundays.

💡 Need a Hint?

Open the Pay Guide. Look under Penalty Rates.

✅ Reveal Answer

Sunday work is usually paid at a higher penalty rate than ordinary hours. The exact percentage depends on your Award and employee classification. Always check the latest Pay Guide.

❄ Task 5 — Find One Allowance

Research one allowance that meat workers may receive.

💡 Need a Hint?

Search the Award for Allowances. Examples include:

  • Cold work allowance
  • Leading hand allowance
  • First aid allowance
  • Protective clothing allowance

✅ Reveal Answer

Possible answers include:

  • Cold work allowance
  • Leading hand allowance
  • First aid allowance
  • Protective clothing allowance
Allowance amounts are updated regularly.

🧾 Task 6 — What Does PAYG Mean?

Research PAYG using the Australian Taxation Office website.

💡 Need a Hint?

Search: ATO PAYG meaning

✅ Reveal Answer

PAYG means Pay As You Go. It is income tax that your employer deducts from your wages and sends to the Australian Taxation Office (ATO).

🏖 Task 7 — What Is Superannuation?

Research what Superannuation is and who pays it.

💡 Need a Hint?

Search: ATO Superannuation

✅ Reveal Answer

Superannuation is money that your employer contributes to your retirement savings fund. It is generally paid in addition to your ordinary wages.

After the Lesson — IELTS Speaking Practice

Part 1 — Introduction and Interview

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

Answer directly → explain → give a personal example.

Yes, I do. / No, I don't. Usually, I ______ because ______. For example, when I ______, I realised ______. Overall, I think ______ because ______.

  • Do you usually check your payslip after you get paid?

    Yes, I do. I always check my payslip to make sure my salary and working hours are correct.

  • Do you think it is important to understand your salary?

    Yes. If you understand your salary, you can check whether you have been paid correctly.

  • Would you ask your employer if you didn't understand your payslip?

    Yes, I would. I think it is better to ask questions than to make incorrect assumptions.

I'd like to talk about a time when ______. At that time, I was working as ______. I received my salary by ______. While checking my payslip, I noticed ______. After that, I ______. This experience taught me that ______.

Sample answer

I'd like to talk about the first time I learned how to read an Australian payslip. Although I wasn't familiar with terms like gross pay, net pay and superannuation, I watched a lesson and asked my teacher to explain them. After that, I understood how my salary is calculated and why tax is deducted before the money goes into my bank account. I think this knowledge is important because every worker should understand how they are paid.

Part 3 — Discussion

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

State your opinion → explain your reason → give an example → conclude.

In my opinion, ______. One reason is ______. For example, ______. However, ______. Overall, I believe ______.

  • Why is it important for employees to understand their payslip?

    Employees should understand their payslip because it helps them check their working hours, deductions and final salary. It also helps them identify mistakes quickly.

  • Why do governments collect income tax?

    Governments collect income tax to pay for public services such as hospitals, schools, roads and emergency services.

  • Should schools teach students about salary and personal finance?

    Yes. Many young people start working without understanding payslips, tax or superannuation. Learning these skills early can help them avoid financial problems.

  • Do you think workers should know their workplace Award?

    Yes. Knowing your Award helps you understand your minimum pay, overtime rates, allowances and workplace rights. It also helps you check whether you are being paid correctly.

  • How has technology changed the way employees receive their salary?

    Today, most employees receive their salary by direct bank transfer and can download electronic payslips online. This is faster and more convenient than paper records.
