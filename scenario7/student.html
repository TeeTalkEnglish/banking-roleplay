Question 1: What is a budget?

A budget is a plan that shows how much money you earn, spend and save.

Question 2: Why should people save money?

People save money for emergencies, future goals and unexpected expenses.

Question 3: What is the difference between needs and wants?

Needs are essential things like food and rent. Wants are things you would like but can live without.

Question 4: What is an emergency fund?

An emergency fund is money saved for unexpected situations like illness, losing a job or repairing your car.

Question 5: Name two ways to save money.

Examples include making a budget, spending less, cooking at home, or setting up automatic savings.

📚 Saving Money Vocabulary

These are common words Australians use when talking about money, budgeting and saving.

📋 Budget

/ˈbʌdʒɪt/

A plan showing how you will spend and save your money.

"I make a weekly budget."

monthly budget budget plan manage a budget

💰 Savings

/ˈseɪvɪŋz/

Money that you keep instead of spending.

"I put money into my savings every payday."

savings account build savings personal savings

💵 Income

/ˈɪnkʌm/

Money you receive from work or other sources.

"My income is paid every fortnight."

weekly income monthly income regular income

🧾 Expenses

/ɪkˈspensɪz/

Money you spend on bills and everyday living.

"My biggest expense is rent."

living expenses monthly expenses household expenses

🚑 Emergency Fund

/ɪˈmɜːdʒənsi fʌnd/

Money saved for unexpected situations.

"I keep an emergency fund."

build an emergency fund emergency savings

📈 Interest

/ˈɪntrəst/

Extra money paid by a bank on your savings.

"My savings earn interest."

interest rate earn interest

🥖 Needs

/niːdz/

Things you must buy to live.

"Food is a need."

basic needs essential needs

🛍 Wants

/wɒnts/

Things you would like but do not really need.

"Coffee every day is a want."

wants vs needs personal wants

🎯 Savings Goal

/ˈseɪvɪŋz ɡəʊl/

An amount of money you want to save.

"My savings goal is $5,000."

reach a goal set a goal

🔄 Automatic Transfer

/ˌɔːtəˈmætɪk ˈtrænsfɜː/

Money automatically moved into another account.

"I set up an automatic transfer every payday."

automatic payment automatic transfer

🏦 Balance

/ˈbæləns/

The amount of money in your bank account.

"My balance is growing."

account balance check balance

💳 Deposit

/dɪˈpɒzɪt/

Money paid into a bank account.

"My salary was deposited today."

bank deposit cash deposit direct deposit

🎓 After the Lesson — IELTS Speaking Practice

Part 1 — Introduction and Interview

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

Answer directly → explain → give one example.

Yes, I do. / No, I don't. Usually, I ______ because ______. For example, ______. Overall, I think ______ because ______.

  • Do you usually save money?

    Yes, I do. I try to save part of my salary every month because I want to prepare for emergencies and future goals.

  • What do you usually spend money on?

    Most of my money goes towards food, rent, transport and my phone bill.

  • Do you prefer saving money or spending money?

    I prefer saving money because it helps me feel more secure and prepared for unexpected expenses.

  • Have you ever bought something you didn't really need?

    Yes, I have. Sometimes I buy coffee or snacks even though I know I should save that money.

Part 2 — Cue Card

(1 minute preparation · 1–2 minutes speaking)

Describe something you are saving money for.

You should say:

  • what you are saving for
  • why it is important
  • how much you need to save
  • and explain how you plan to reach your goal.
Answer template

I'd like to talk about ______. I'm saving money because ______. I need approximately ______. Every week I ______. I also try to ______. I believe I'll reach my goal because ______.

Sample answer

I'd like to talk about saving money for a new laptop. I need a good computer because I want to study cybersecurity and work online. The laptop costs about $2,000. Every payday I transfer $200 into my savings account before spending anything else. I also cook at home and avoid buying unnecessary things. I think I'll reach my goal within one year if I stay disciplined.

Part 3 — Discussion

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

State your opinion → explain → give an example → conclude.

In my opinion, ______. One reason is ______. For example, ______. However, ______. Overall, I believe ______.

  • Why do some people find it difficult to save money?

    Many people spend money on things they want instead of things they need. Some people also don't have a budget, so they don't know where their money goes.

  • Should schools teach students about money management?

    Yes. Learning how to budget, save money and avoid debt is an important life skill that everyone should learn before they start working.

  • Why do banks encourage customers to save money?

    Banks encourage saving because customers keep their money in the bank, and some savings accounts also earn interest over time.

  • Do you think young people save enough money today?

    Some do, but many spend too much on shopping, entertainment and online purchases. Building good saving habits early is very important.
