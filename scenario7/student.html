Every fortnight your salary arrives in your bank account.

At first, you spend your money on almost everything:

  • ☕ Coffee every morning

  • 👕 New clothes
  • 📱 Online shopping

📚 Saving Money Vocabulary

These are common words Australians use when talking about saving money and budgeting.

🏦 Savings Account

/ˈseɪ.vɪŋz əˈkaʊnt/

A bank account used to save money and earn interest.

"I put $200 into my savings account every payday."

high-interest savings account open a savings account transfer to savings

📋 Budget

/ˈbʌdʒɪt/

A plan that shows how you spend and save your money.

"I follow a weekly budget."

make a budget monthly budget budget planner

💸 Expense

/ɪkˈspens/

Money you spend.

"My biggest expense is rent."

living expenses daily expenses unexpected expenses

💵 Income

/ˈɪnkʌm/

Money you receive from your job.

"My income is paid every fortnight."

monthly income weekly income stable income

🚑 Emergency Fund

/ɪˈmɜːdʒənsi fʌnd/

Money saved for unexpected situations.

"My emergency fund helped pay for my car repairs."

build an emergency fund emergency savings

📈 Interest

/ˈɪntrəst/

Extra money the bank pays you for keeping money in your savings account.

"My bank pays interest every month."

interest rate earn interest high interest

🏠 Fixed Expense

/fɪkst ɪkˈspens/

A payment that is usually the same every month.

"My rent is a fixed expense."

fixed cost monthly rent

🛒 Variable Expense

/ˈveəriəbl ɪkˈspens/

A payment that changes from month to month.

"My food expenses change every week."

variable costs food expenses

🎯 Financial Goal

/faɪˈnænʃəl ɡəʊl/

Something you want to save money for.

"My financial goal is to buy a car."

set a goal reach your goal

⬇ Deposit

/dɪˈpɒzɪt/

To put money into your bank account.

"I deposited $300 into my savings."

cash deposit bank deposit

⬆ Withdraw

/wɪðˈdrɔː/

To take money out of your bank account.

"I withdrew $50 from the ATM."

withdraw cash ATM withdrawal

🤖 Automatic Savings

/ˌɔːtəˈmætɪk ˈseɪvɪŋz/

Money automatically transferred into your savings account.

"I use automatic savings every payday."

automatic transfer save automatically

📊 Australia's 50 / 30 / 20 Budget Rule

Many financial experts recommend the 50/30/20 budgeting rule.

50%

🏠 Needs
Rent, food, transport, bills

30%

🎉 Wants
Shopping, hobbies, eating out

20%

💰 Savings
Emergency fund & future goals

Why do many Australians use this rule?

It helps people balance everyday living, enjoy life responsibly, and still save money for the future.
