🏦 Opening a Bank Account
Week 1 		You need an account to get paid. There are 3 options. You don't know which is cheapest. Some have monthly fees — but you don't know how much. Choose between:
Everyday Account
Student Account
Basic Account
You must ask the bank worker which is best for you.
arvo no worries reckon sorted
💬 Slang help

"No worries" = It's fine / don't stress

"Sorted" = Finished / set up

"Reckon" = Think / believe
fees? monthly? free? interest? locked? overdraft?
Ask which account has no monthly fee. Ask what happens if your balance drops too low. Don't accept the first option!
$
If you pick the wrong account: pay $5–$8/month in hidden fees = $60–$96/year wasted
💡 Helpful phrases
"Are there any monthly account-keeping fees?"Ask this first — very direct, normal in Aus
"What happens if I go into overdraft?"Overdraft = spending more than you have
"Which account is best for someone just starting out?"Bank workers hear this every day

"Heaps" = A lot / very

"Dodgy" = Suspicious / not trustworthy

☎️ Suspicious Phone Call
Week 3 		Someone calls claiming to be from the ATO (Tax Office). They say you owe money and must pay immediately or face arrest. Decide: Is this real? Do you hang up? Do you pay? How do you respond calmly in English?
no way sketchy full on hang on
scam?ID?callback?verify?
 Stay calm. Ask for their name and ID number. Say you'll call back on the official number. Don't give personal details or pay. — If you pay: lost money — unrecoverable. ATO NEVER demands immediate payment by phone.
💡 What to say
"Can I get your name and employee ID please?"Scammers often hang up at this point
"I'll call the ATO directly on 13 28 61 to verify this."The official ATO number — always safe to say this
"I'm not comfortable giving my details over the phone."100% legitimate thing to say in Australia

💸 International Money Transfer
Week 4 		You want to send $200 home to your family. Your bank says they can do it. But you don't know the exchange rate, fees, or how long it takes. Decide: use the bank, or use Wise/Western Union? Ask about fees and exchange rates before sending.
reckon fair go dodgy
rate?fee?delay?arrive?better?
 Ask the bank about the exact exchange rate AND transfer fee. Ask how long it takes. Compare before deciding.
$
Banks charge $15–$30 transfer fee PLUS bad exchange rates. Could cost $40+ extra vs Wise/Remitly.
💡 Phrases
"What exchange rate do you use — is it the mid-market rate?"Banks often use a worse rate to make profit
"Is there a transfer fee on top of that?"Always ask — fees can be $20–$35
"How long does it take to arrive in [country]?"Can be 1–5 business days

📖 Banking Vocabulary Survival Kit

🏦 Banking Terms

Overdraft — spending more than you have
Direct debit — automatic bill payment
BSB — bank sort code (6 digits)
PayID — send money with phone/email
Transaction account — everyday spending account
Offset account — savings that reduce loan interest

🏠 Renting Terms

Bond — security deposit (max 4 weeks)
Lease — rental contract
Body corp — apartment building management
RTBA — holds your bond safely
Condition report — document listing damage
Outgoings — extra fees (strata, rates)

🛡️ Scam Red Flags

Urgent — real services don't rush you
Wire transfer — scammer's favourite method
Gift cards — government never asks for these
.ru / .cn links — not Australian banks
"You'll be arrested" — ATO/police don't say this
