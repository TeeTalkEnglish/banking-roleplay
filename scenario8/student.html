🥩 Meat Industry Vocabulary

These words are commonly used by meat processing workers in Australia.

🥩 Shift

/ʃɪft/

A scheduled period of work.

"I work the morning shift."

📅 Roster

/ˈrɒs.tər/

Your weekly work schedule.

"My roster changes every week."

⏰ Penalty Rate

/ˈpen.əl.ti reɪt/

Extra pay for weekends or public holidays.

"I receive penalty rates on Sundays."

❄ Cold Room

/kəʊld ruːm/

A refrigerated work area.

"I work in the cold room."

👷 Supervisor

/ˈsuː.pə.vaɪ.zər/

The person who manages workers.

"My supervisor approved my leave."

☕ Break

/breɪk/

A short rest during work.

"We have a 30-minute lunch break."
