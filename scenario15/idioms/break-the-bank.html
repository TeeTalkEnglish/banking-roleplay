📖 Meaning

Break the Bank means something costs so much money that you cannot afford it, or it would use almost all of your money.

🏦 Why is it called "Break the Bank"?

Many years ago, people played gambling games in casinos.

If one player won so much money that the casino could not pay everyone, they were said to break the bank.

Today, the idiom usually means something is extremely expensive.

🛍 Real World Examples

  • 💻 A $5,000 gaming computer
  • 🚗 Buying a luxury car
  • 🏠 Purchasing a house
  • ✈ Booking a first-class flight

If something costs nearly all your money, you can say it would break the bank.

Example Sentences

✅ That designer handbag would break the bank.
✅ We found a nice hotel that didn't break the bank.
✅ Buying a new car won't break the bank.
✅ This phone looks expensive, but it won't break the bank.
✅ Let's find a restaurant that won't break the bank.
✅ Their holiday didn't break the bank because they planned carefully.

💼 Business Tip

Businesses often advertise affordable products by saying they "won't break the bank."

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is "Break the Bank" positive?

Usually yes. It often means something is affordable when used as "won't break the bank."

Can I use it when shopping?

Yes. Native speakers use it all the time when talking about prices.

What is the opposite?

Something affordable or inexpensive.
