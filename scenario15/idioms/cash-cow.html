💰 Cash Cow

Category: Banking Idioms / Business English

Meaning

A cash cow is a business, product or investment that makes a lot of money consistently with very little effort or cost.

Pronunciation

/kæʃ kaʊ/

Example Sentences

✅ Our mobile app has become a cash cow for the company.
✅ Coffee is the café's cash cow.
✅ That product has been a cash cow for over ten years.

Conversation

Person A Person B
Which product earns the most money? Our online banking service is our biggest cash cow.

Business Tip

Companies often use the profits from a cash cow to invest in new products.

Common Mistake

❌ The cow gives lots of cash.

✅ Cash cow means a profitable business.

Quick Quiz

Which one is a cash cow?
  1. A business losing money
  2. A product making steady profits ✅
  3. A company with many employees
  4. A farm with cows
