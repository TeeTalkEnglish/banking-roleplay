📖 Meaning

A Cash Cow is a product, service, or business that earns steady profits over many years.

🐄 Why is it called a Cash Cow?

Imagine a farmer who owns a healthy cow.

Every day the cow produces milk.

The farmer sells the milk and earns money.

The cow keeps producing milk every day.

Businesses work the same way.

Some products continue making money for years with very little extra effort.

Those products are called Cash Cows.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is "Cash Cow" a positive expression?

Yes. It describes a business, product, or service that generates reliable profits.

Can I use "Cash Cow" in everyday conversation?

Yes. It's common in business discussions, marketing, investing, and workplace English.

What is the opposite of a Cash Cow?

A product or business that consistently loses money rather than making a profit.
