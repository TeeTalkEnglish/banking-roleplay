Day 5: Work, Salary & Getting Paid

Learn how to talk about money, salary, work payments, and solve common money problems in Australia.

💵 New Vocabulary You Will Learn Today

These words are commonly used at work, in payroll, and at Australian banks.

Word Meaning Example
Salary Fixed regular payment My salary is paid monthly.
Wage Payment by hour My wage is $30 per hour.
Payslip Document showing earnings Can you explain my payslip?
Gross Pay Pay before deductions My gross pay seems correct.
Net Pay Money received after deductions My net pay looks lower.
Tax Money paid to government Why was tax deducted?
Payroll Department handling employee pay I contacted payroll.
Superannuation Australian retirement savings Has my super been paid?
BSB Australian bank branch number Where do I find my BSB?
Direct Deposit Money sent into account automatically I receive direct deposit.
Processing Time Time needed for payment How long is processing?
Deduction Money removed from pay What deductions are included?

Scenario 5A — First Pay Didn’t Arrive

Your Role

You worked your first week at a café. Payday passed but your money has not arrived.

Your Objective

  • Ask what happened.
  • Check if your details are correct.
  • Find out when you will get paid.

Vocabulary

payday • payroll • direct deposit • processing • pending

Hint Try asking:
  • I haven't received my pay yet.
  • Can you check my payment?
  • When should it arrive?

Scenario 5B — Understanding Your Payslip

Your Role

You received less money than expected.

Your Objective

  • Understand deductions.
  • Understand tax.
  • Understand your final pay.

Vocabulary

gross pay • net pay • tax • deduction • super

Hint Ask:
  • Why is my pay lower?
  • Can you explain my payslip?

Scenario 5C — Salary Deposit Setup

Your Role

Your manager needs your bank information.

Your Objective

  • Provide correct payment details.
  • Understand where salary goes.

Vocabulary

BSB • payroll • account number • salary

Hint Ask:
  • Where can I find my BSB?
  • Do you need my account number?

Scenario 5D — Unexpected Bank Fee

Your Role

You noticed missing money in your account.

Your Objective

  • Understand charges.
  • Learn how to avoid fees.

Vocabulary

account fee • statement • transaction • monthly fee

Hint Ask:
  • Why was I charged?
  • Can I avoid this fee?

Real-Life Money Vocabulary

  • Salary → Fixed regular pay
  • Wage → Hourly payment
  • Payslip → Record of earnings
  • Overtime → Extra work hours
  • Tax → Money paid to government
  • Superannuation → Retirement savings
  • Payroll → Company payment system
  • Payday → Day employees get paid

Useful Workplace Questions

  • I haven't received my pay.
  • Can you explain my payslip?
  • Why is my pay lower?
  • How do I update my bank details?
  • Where can I find my BSB?

Reflection

  • What new money vocabulary did you learn?
  • Which situation felt most realistic?
  • Would you feel confident discussing pay in Australia?
