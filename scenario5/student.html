Day 5

💰 My First Payday in Australia

You have started your first job. Learn how to understand your payslip, check whether your salary arrived, provide your bank details, and ask about bank fees.

🎯 Today's Mission

  1. Understand your payslip.
  2. Check whether your salary arrived.
  3. Provide your bank details.
  4. Ask about unexpected bank fees.

🇦🇺 Australian Tip

Many workers in Australia are paid every fortnight by direct deposit. Employers usually need your BSB and account number.

Scenario 5A — My Salary Didn’t Arrive

Your Mission

You expected your salary today, but it is not in your account yet.

Useful Expressions

I haven’t received my salary yet.
Could you check my payment?
When should it arrive?
