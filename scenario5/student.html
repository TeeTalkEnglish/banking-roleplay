🇦🇺 Australian Tip

After the Roleplay — IELTS Speaking Practice

Part 1 — Introduction and Interview

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

Answer directly → explain → add an example.

Yes, I have. / No, not really. Usually, I try to ___ because ___. For example, when I received ___, I decided to ___. Overall, I think ___ because ___.

  • Have you ever had a problem with a bank transaction?

    Yes, I have. Once my salary did not arrive on time, so I called the bank to check the payment.

  • How do you feel when you check your bank account?

    I feel careful and focused. I want to make sure all payments are correct and nothing is missing.

  • What do you do if you see a bank fee you do not understand?

    I ask the bank for an explanation and compare the charge with my account details.

Part 2 — Cue Card

(1 min preparation · 1–2 mins speaking)

Describe a time when you had to ask about your salary, payslip, or a bank fee.

  • what happened
  • why you were worried
  • what you did
  • and explain how it ended
Answer template

I'd like to talk about a time when ___. This happened when ___. I felt worried because ___. To deal with the situation, I first ___. After that, I ___. In the end, ___. Looking back, I learned ___.

Sample answer

I'd like to talk about a time when my salary did not arrive on payday. I felt worried because I needed the money to pay bills. I contacted payroll and the bank. In the end, I found out the payment was delayed because the employer used the wrong account number, and the bank corrected it.

Part 3 — Discussion

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

State opinion → explain → compare → example.

In my opinion, ___. One reason is ___. For example, ___. However, ___. Overall, ___.

  • Why is it important to understand your payslip?

    It is important because the payslip shows your gross pay, deductions, and net pay. This helps you know if your salary is correct.

  • How can banks help customers avoid unexpected fees?

    Banks can provide clearer information and send notifications before charging a fee.

  • Should people ask for help when they are not sure about bank information?

    Yes, they should. It is better to ask and clarify than to make a mistake with money.
