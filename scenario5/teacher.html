Day 5 — Work, Salary & Getting Paid

Teach students how to solve realistic salary, banking and payroll situations in Australia.

Scenario 5A — First Pay Didn’t Arrive

Expected Discovery

  • Employer entered incorrect bank details
  • Payroll processes every Thursday
  • Bank may take 1–2 business days

Teacher Responses

"Let me confirm your BSB and account number."

"The payment was submitted yesterday."

"It may still be processing."

SECRET CHALLENGE: Student accidentally provided wrong BSB.

Scenario 5B — Understanding Payslip

Explain

  • Gross Pay → before deductions
  • Net Pay → actual money received
  • Tax → government deduction
  • Super → retirement savings

Expected Student Questions

  • Why is my pay lower?
  • What is this deduction?
  • What is super?
SECRET CHALLENGE: Student forgot to submit TFN.

Scenario 5C — Salary Deposit Setup

Required Details

  • Account Name
  • BSB
  • Account Number

Teacher Notes

Explain that BSB identifies the bank branch.

SECRET CHALLENGE: Manager entered wrong account number.

Scenario 5D — Unexpected Bank Fee

Possible Causes

  • Monthly account fee
  • ATM fee
  • International transaction fee

Teacher Questions

  • When did you notice it?
  • Can you show your statement?
SECRET CHALLENGE: Fee caused by overseas payment.

Australian Workplace Money Guide

  • Salary → fixed payment
  • Wage → hourly pay
  • Payslip → payment breakdown
  • Payroll → payment department
  • Superannuation → retirement fund
  • TFN → Tax File Number

Assessment Checklist

  • □ Greeting professionally
  • □ Asking clarification questions
  • □ Using money vocabulary
  • □ Solving workplace problems
  • □ Completing objective
  • □ Speaking naturally
