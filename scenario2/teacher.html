Scenario 2 - Teacher

Lost Debit Card Teller Guide

Use this page to play the bank teller and prepare the learner for a real Australian banking problem: a missing card, possible fraud, identity checks, app safety, temporary money access and replacement-card delays.

Teacher setup

Student role: A customer in Australia whose debit card is missing.

Teacher role: A bank teller who asks verification questions, checks recent transactions and guides the customer through the safest next step.

Do not give the full solution immediately. Let the learner ask questions, then reveal realistic information based on the challenge you choose.

Realistic problem — Address mismatch Old address on file, new share house needed. Ask for proof of the new address before ordering the card. No change to transactions. "I can order the card, but your address is different on our system. Can you provide proof of your new address?"

Correction targets

  • Use "lost my debit card", not "lost my bank".
  • Use "block", "freeze" or "cancel" for the card action.
  • Use "transaction" for a payment shown on the account.
  • Use "I don't recognise this payment", not "I don't know this money".
  • Use polite direct questions: "Can you...?", "Could I...?", "How long...?"

Debrief answers

  • Freeze or block the card as soon as possible.
  • Check recent transactions and identify anything unknown.
  • Cancel the card if it might be stolen or compromised.
  • Ask the bank how to access money while waiting.
  • Never share PINs, passwords or one-time codes.
  • Keep address and phone details updated with the bank.
