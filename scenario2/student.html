Scenario 2

Lost Debit Card

You are in Australia and your debit card is missing. Tell the bank teller what you know, ask them to make the card safe, and check any payments you do not recognise.

Key words

Freeze / block Stop the card so nobody can use it. Suspicious transaction A payment that might not be yours. Dispute Ask the bank to investigate a payment. Replacement card A new card after the old one is lost or cancelled.

Your role

Name: Mina Tran

Account: Everyday transaction account

Location: Melbourne, Australia

Job: Meat butcher

ID: Passport photo ID and work ID card

Problem: Your debit card is missing and your phone number recently changed, so the bank app code may not work.

Start the conversation

Customer: Good morning. I think I've lost my debit card.

Teller: I'm sorry to hear that. When did you last have it?

Customer: I used it yesterday afternoon at Woolworths Melbourne Central.

Customer: Can you freeze the card and check for suspicious transactions?

Information to use

Only share details when the teller asks. Use short, clear answers.

Remember

  • Do not share your PIN, password or one-time security code.
  • Say clearly which payments you recognise and which you do not recognise.
  • Ask what happens next before you leave the bank.
  • Ask for a reference number before you finish.

After the roleplay

  • What should you do first when a debit card is missing?
  • Which transactions did Mina recognise?
  • Which transactions should Mina dispute?
  • How can Mina pay for important things while waiting for a new card?
