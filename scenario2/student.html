Scenario 2

Lost Debit Card

You are in Australia and your debit card is missing. Tell the bank teller what you know, ask them to make the card safe, and check any payments you do not recognise.

Key words

Freeze / block Stop the card so nobody can use it. Suspicious transaction A payment that might not be yours. Dispute Ask the bank to investigate a payment. Replacement card A new card after the old one is lost or cancelled.

Your role

Name: Mina Tran

Account: Everyday transaction account

Location: Melbourne, Australia

Job: Meat butcher

ID: Passport photo ID and work ID card

Problem: Your debit card is missing and your phone number recently changed, so the bank app code may not work.

Start the conversation

Customer: Good morning. I think I've lost my debit card.

Teller: I'm sorry to hear that. When did you last have it?

Customer: I used it yesterday afternoon at Woolworths Melbourne Central.

Customer: Can you freeze the card and check for suspicious transactions?

Information to use

Only share details when the teller asks. Use short, clear answers.

Remember

  • Do not share your PIN, password or one-time security code.
  • Say clearly which payments you recognise and which you do not recognise.
  • Ask what happens next before you leave the bank.
  • Ask for a reference number before you finish.

📘 Learn Before Speaking

Review this short guide before answering:

IDP IELTS — Adverbs of Frequency

Focus on: usually • often • sometimes • rarely • never

After the roleplay

Part 1 - Introduction and interview

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

Give a direct answer. Add one reason. Add one small example.

"I usually __ because __. For example, __."

  • Do you usually pay by cash, card or phone? Why?

    I usually pay by card because it is quick and I do not need to carry much cash. For example, I use my card when I buy groceries or take public transport.

  • Have you ever had a problem with a bank card or payment?

    Yes, once my card payment did not go through at a shop. I checked my banking app, tried again, and then used another payment method.

  • Do you think it is easy to use banking apps? Why or why not?

    I think banking apps are quite easy if people use them regularly. However, they can be confusing for older people or anyone who is not confident with technology.

Part 2 - Cue card

(1–2 mins speaking, 1 min prep)

Describe a time when you lost something important.

  • what you lost
  • where you think you lost it
  • what you did next
  • and explain how you felt about the situation.
Answer template

Start with the situation. Explain the details in order. Finish with your feeling or lesson.

"I'd like to talk about a time when I lost __. It happened __. First, I __. Then, I __. In the end, __. I felt __ because __."

Sample answer

I'd like to talk about a time when I lost my debit card. I think I left it at a supermarket after paying for food. First, I checked my wallet and bag carefully. Then, I called the bank and asked them to freeze the card. In the end, I got a replacement card. I felt worried at first, but I was relieved after the bank made the card safe.

Part 3 - Discussion

(4–5 mins)

Answer template

Give your opinion. Explain the reason. Compare two sides or give a wider example.

"In my opinion, __. The main reason is __. However, __. For example, __."

  • Why do some people worry about card payments or online banking?

    Some people worry because they are afraid of fraud or suspicious transactions. They may also worry about losing their phone, forgetting passwords, or making a mistake online.

  • Do you think digital payments are safer than cash? Why or why not?

    In my opinion, digital payments can be safer because banks can block a lost card and track transactions. However, cash can feel simpler because it does not need a phone, internet connection, or password.

  • How can banks help customers feel more confident when they report a lost or stolen card?

    Banks can help by giving clear instructions, quick support, and simple ways to report a lost card. For example, they could explain what will happen next and give the customer a reference number.
